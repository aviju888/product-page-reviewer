Made for the long way round

The Trail Bottle is built for days that start before sunrise and end after dark. Two walls of 18/8 stainless steel with a vacuum between them keep ice frozen through a hot afternoon, and the outside never sweats onto your maps or your laptop.

The chug lid opens with a thumb and seals with a quarter turn, so you can drink while you walk and toss it in a pack without a second thought. A powder coat grips in wet hands and shrugs off scrapes from granite and car keys alike.

We test every batch for leaks and temperature before it ships, and we stand behind it for life. If the steel ever fails, we replace it.

Questions, answered

Does it fit in a car cup holder?

The 500 ml does. The 750 ml fits most wide cup holders; the 1 L does not.

Can I put hot drinks in it?

Yes. It keeps drinks hot for 12 hours. Open the lid slowly with very hot liquids.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Top rack only. Hand washing keeps the powder coat looking new longer.

What does the warranty cover?

Any failure of the steel or the vacuum seal, for life. Email us a photo and we ship a new one.
